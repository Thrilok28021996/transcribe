ACTIVE TIER: CONFIRMING

The autonomy tier is a ceiling enforced in code. No confidence score can promote an action beyond your configured ceiling.

❓ Asking

"What should I do here?" — Always asks for your explicit instruction before performing any action.

⚠️ Confirming

"I'm about to do X — ok?" — Prepares actions and presents 1-click confirmation cards.

⚡ Acting

"Handled it; here's what I did" — Executes autonomous tasks and logs to audit ledger.
